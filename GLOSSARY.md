# Learning System

A mentoring and interview-coaching method, plus the learner's own record of what they have studied
and still need to review.

## Curriculum

**Track**:
One technology taught as its own curriculum, such as Java + Spring Boot or React.
_Avoid_: Course, subject

**Section**:
A named group of topics inside a track, one `## Track …` heading in its curriculum.
_Avoid_: Module, chapter, folder

**Topic**:
One row of a curriculum: the unit you learn, finish, and review.
_Avoid_: Lesson (a lesson is the session in which you study a topic), item

**Part**:
A piece of a topic taught on its own, named by a trailing letter (`02a`, `02b`).
_Avoid_: Subtopic, sub-lesson

## Lessons and their output

**Step**:
One of the five stages of a lesson defined by the methodology.
_Avoid_: Using it for a click-through screen inside a figure (that is a frame)

**Figure**:
An interactive drawing that explains one topic or part.
_Avoid_: Diagram, illustration, visual

**Frame**:
One click-through state of a figure: what it shows after a given press of Next.
_Avoid_: Step, slide, page

**Summary card**:
The short written recap of a topic, made at the end of its lesson.
_Avoid_: Notes, recap

**Cheat sheet**:
The combined recap of several topics, made at the end of a scratch pass.
_Avoid_: Summary (that is one topic's card)
